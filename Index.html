We keep your public life organised, so you can stay creative.

Fanconnect is a media management agency in Barcelona. We handle the daily work around a public profile: what gets posted, who gets answered, which journalists get a call and which offers are worth your time.

  • One point of contact. A single manager who knows your schedule, your voice and your limits.
  • Fans first. Real replies, real community, no bought followers.
  • Clear reporting. A monthly summary of reach, press and revenue in plain language.

What we plan to offer

Six services you can combine into a monthly retainer or book one at a time.

Social media management

Content calendars, posting, replies and monthly analytics across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and X.

Press and PR

Press releases, interview requests, media kits and relationships with local and national outlets.

Fan community

Moderated fan groups, newsletters, giveaways and meet-and-greet organisation.

Content production

Short video, photo shoots and editing, planned around your brand and shot in Barcelona.

Brand partnerships

We find, negotiate and manage sponsorship deals that fit your audience and values.

Bookings and appearances

Events, festivals, panels and signings: enquiries, contracts, travel and on-the-day coordination.
